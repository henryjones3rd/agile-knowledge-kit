# Sprint reviews

A sprint review is a working session, not a status report. Stakeholders see
finished work and help decide what comes next.

## Agenda (45 minutes)

1. **Sprint goal and outcome** (5 min): Did we meet it? One sentence.
2. **Demo of finished work** (20 min): Only items that meet the definition of done.
3. **Feedback** (10 min): What should change based on what you saw?
4. **Look ahead** (10 min): Top items for next sprint and any needed
   [Feature Launch Reservations](feature-launch-reservations.md).

## Rules

- Demo working software, not slides.
- The people who built it present it.
- Unfinished work is not demoed. Say so plainly and move on.
