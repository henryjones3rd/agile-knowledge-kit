# Feature Launch Reservations

A **Feature Launch Reservation (FLR)** is a block of time a team books with the
teams it depends on, during quarterly planning, before the quarter starts.

## The analogy

It works like a restaurant reservation. You book the table ahead of time. You do
not need to know what you will order. That part emerges during the meal.

Without a reservation, teams scramble mid-quarter over chat, hoping someone is
available. It is like trying to get a table on a Friday night without calling
ahead.

## What a reservation looks like

> Sprint 2, first two days, 09:30–11:30 on a video call. It is on the calendar
> before the quarter starts.

## How it works

1. During quarterly planning, each team lists the features that depend on
   another team.
2. The teams agree on reservation windows for those launches.
3. During a window, the supporting team joins the call **with work they can
   pick up and put down**.
4. If nothing goes wrong, they keep working on their own items. If something
   unexpected happens, the right people are already together with full context.

## Anticipated pushback

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "We already have a chat group for features." | Good. You have the restaurant's number. Now call ahead and book the table. |
| "How can we reserve if we don't know the details yet?" | You don't order your meal when you book a table. You just need to know you'll be hungry. |
| "What if they aren't needed?" | They bring interruptible work. Nothing is wasted. |
| "I'll just reach out to my contact." | Relationships are great, but a favor isn't a plan. What happens when that person is on vacation? |

## When you don't need one

Small configuration changes and low-risk updates can launch without a
reservation. Use the [reservation decision guide](../decision-guides/does-this-need-a-reservation.md).
