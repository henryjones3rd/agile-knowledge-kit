# Reservation request template

Copy this into your planning board or calendar invite.

```markdown
## Feature Launch Reservation

- Feature: <name and link to backlog item>
- Requesting team: <team>
- Supporting team: <team>
- Window: Sprint <n>, <dates>, <start>–<end> <time zone>
- Call link: <link>
- Why this team: <system or expertise you may need>
- Rollback plan: <one line>
- Reminder: bring work you can pick up and put down
```

See [Feature Launch Reservations](../playbooks/feature-launch-reservations.md)
for how reservations work.
